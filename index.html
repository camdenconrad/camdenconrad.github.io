Camden Conrad

I'm a software engineer and systems programmer in Troutman, North Carolina, working toward flight software — specifically mission design and GNC. The goal is to work on things that actually solve hard problems: trajectory optimization, orbital mechanics, guidance, navigation, and control.

Largely self-taught. I studied computer science at UNC Charlotte (2021–2026, AI / Robotics / Game Dev concentration) — completed the CS core and a senior project, but chose not to finish the degree. My work centers on determinism, zero-GC and manual memory management, and data-oriented design: software written as if it assumes a bit flipped somewhere.

Alongside engineering I have a strong music-production background and was previously signed to a label — useful range for the audio and tooling problems that keep appearing in my projects.
